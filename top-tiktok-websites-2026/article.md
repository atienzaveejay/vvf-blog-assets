Top 10 Websites to Find Viral TikTok Videos and Trends in 2026

Finding a trend early is useful. Finding a format that already worked for a creator in your niche is often more useful. The best websites to find viral TikTok videos help you move from random scrolling to structured research: discover strong examples, understand why they worked, and adapt the idea without copying it.

No trend tool can guarantee that your next post will go viral. Use these platforms as research aids, then rely on your execution, audience fit, and testing.

1. Viral Video Finder (VVF)

Best for: studying breakout organic videos and turning them into usable creative ideas.

VVF's TikTok dashboard lets you narrow the video feed by posted-within period, follower-count range, and category, then sort results by Outlier Score or views. Its Outlier multiplier compares a video's views with its Audience Base, revealing videos that reached far beyond the creator's existing audience.

Each video can be opened in Video Analysis, where VVF provides an AI summary, a Why It Went Viral breakdown, first-three-second hook analysis with a score and hook ideas, a copyable timestamped transcript, and a Creative Strategist covering format, tone, pacing, what to show, and what to say. The Create your own script workflow turns that research into an editable draft with regeneration and script history. Bookmarks save videos, while Analysis history keeps previous research accessible.

Plan details can change, so check Viral Video Finder's current account and pricing pages before subscribing.

2. TikTok Creative Center

Best for: official TikTok trend and creative data.

TikTok Creative Center includes trend pages for hashtags, songs, creators, and videos, plus ad inspiration and creative guidance. Because it is TikTok's own resource, it is a strong place to validate whether a sound or topic is active in a particular market. It shows what is already trending; it does not promise future performance.

H2
3. Exolyt

Best for: TikTok social listening and organic analytics.

Exolyt focuses on organic TikTok content, including accounts, videos, hashtags, sounds, sentiment, brand monitoring, and trends. It is better suited to teams that need deeper market or competitor research than creators who only want a quick idea feed.

H2
4. Pentos

Best for: tracking TikTok songs, users, topics, and trends.

Pentos provides global trend lists and tracking for accounts and content. It is especially useful for music teams, brands, and agencies monitoring how a sound or topic changes over time.

H2
5. TrendTok

Best for: mobile trend discovery.

TrendTok is a mobile app rather than a traditional desktop research site. It helps users discover and track TikTok trends by location, category, or creator. Treat its predictions as directional signals, then confirm the trend inside TikTok before producing content.

H2
6. Google Trends

Best for: checking wider search interest.

Google Trends does not measure TikTok views. It shows relative Google search interest over time. Use it to determine whether a topic is growing beyond TikTok, compare wording, and spot seasonal demand.

H2
7. Exploding Topics

Best for: finding broader topics before they become mainstream.

Exploding Topics monitors wider web signals rather than TikTok alone. It can help you find emerging products, behaviors, and categories that may inspire short-form content. You still need a TikTok-specific tool to validate the actual video format.

H2
8. Later's TikTok Trends Roundup

Best for: curated examples and quick creative prompts.

Later regularly publishes TikTok trend roundups with examples and ideas for adapting sounds or formats. It is useful when you want a human explanation without opening an analytics dashboard.

H2
9. New Engen TikTok Trends

Best for: weekly trend summaries for brands.

New Engen tracks breakout sounds, formats, and cultural moments, with notes on how brands can participate. This is editorial trend curation, not a complete database, but it can save research time.

H2
10. Social Blade

Best for: checking account growth.

Social Blade tracks public account statistics and growth history. It is not a TikTok trend predictor. Use it to see whether a creator is gaining momentum, then inspect the creator's recent posts to identify possible drivers.

Which TikTok Trend Tool Should You Choose?

Choose VVF when you want organic video examples, outlier-based discovery, and a creative breakdown you can turn into a script. Use TikTok Creative Center when you need first-party trend validation. Choose Exolyt or Pentos for deeper monitoring. Use Google Trends or Exploding Topics to validate broader demand. Keep Later and New Engen bookmarked for quick editorial inspiration.

A Simple Three-Step Research Workflow

1. Discover: browse VVF for unusually strong videos in a relevant category and save three references.
2. Validate: check TikTok Creative Center and Google Trends to confirm that the sound, topic, or audience interest is still active.
3. Adapt: use the VVF hook analysis, transcript, and delivery guidance to build an original version for your audience. Copy the underlying structure, not the creator's wording or footage.

Frequently Asked Questions

H3
Can a TikTok trend finder guarantee viral views?
No. These tools reveal patterns and current signals. Results still depend on the idea, execution, relevance, timing, and audience response.

H3
What is the best free place to find TikTok trends?
TikTok Creative Center and Google Trends are useful free starting points. VVF is the stronger fit when you want one workflow that connects video discovery, performance context, AI analysis, bookmarks, and script creation.

H3
Is an Outlier Score the same as predicting virality?
No. VVF's Outlier multiplier is based on views relative to Audience Base. For example, a video with 173,900 views and an Audience Base of 5 can show an outlier of roughly 34,780×. It describes the scale of an existing result; it does not predict future virality.
